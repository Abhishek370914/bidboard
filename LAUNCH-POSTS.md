## Seeding (before posting publicly)

Ask 5–10 founder friends to place REAL first bids ($10–$25) on their own
products in the hour before the X post, so the board isn't empty when
traffic lands. Never bid on your own board and present it as demand —
the revenue counter only counts real settled payments, keep it that way.

## After posting

- Screenshot the board + revenue counter whenever #1 changes hands;
  quote-post it. Every screenshot of the board is free distribution —
  that's the loop that carried the original.
- Reply to every bidder publicly ("new #1: X at $Y — outbid them by $5").
- If 48 hours pass with no bids beyond the seed group, the honest read
